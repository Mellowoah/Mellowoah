How to play

  • Throw downYour phone is your hand. Swipe up (away from you), tap, or press Throw and the yo-yo flies out toward the floor. Angle the swipe for a Breakaway.
  • Swing for tricksWhile it sleeps, drag to move your hand. Swing wide for Whip and Around the World, rock 3× for Rock the Baby, drag up to reach it to the floor and Walk the Dog.
  • Bind it homePull down toward you, tap, or press Bind while the spin is hot. Tricks in one run stack a combo, and points earn XP and coins.
  • Tilt mode (phones)Tilt to move your hand. Flick the phone forward to throw and pull it back toward you to bind. Touch always works too.
  • Carnival platesPlates lie on the floor below you. Move your hand to aim, then drop a full sleeper, swing low or Walk the Dog across them to smash them. Small plates score more, gold plates are a bonus, and quick chains multiply.
