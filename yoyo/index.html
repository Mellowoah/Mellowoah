How to play

  • ThrowTap, flick down, or press Throw. Side-flick for a Breakaway.
  • Swing & tricksWhile it sleeps, drag to move your hand. Swing wide for Whip and Around the World, rock 3× for Rock the Baby, lower it to the floor to Walk the Dog.
  • Bind it homeTap, flick up, or press Bind while the spin is hot for a clean bind. Each trick in a run raises the multiplier.
  • Tilt mode (phones)Tilt to move your hand, snap your wrist to throw and bind. Touch always works too.
