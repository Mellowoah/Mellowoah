# Yo (fixed build, v3)

A self-contained static app: `index.html` (all JS and CSS inline, including the SVG logo), plus `assets/` (app icons at 180, 192, 512 and 1024 px, and `og.png`, the 1200×630 social preview), `favicon.svg` and `manifest.webmanifest`. There is no build step and nothing to install. The only network request is Google Fonts (Lilita One, Fraunces, Outfit), with system fonts as the fallback.

Run it locally with `python3 -m http.server` in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000.
On phones, tilt controls and code redeeming need HTTPS (or localhost): iOS only grants motion permission on secure origins, and `crypto.subtle` only exists there.

## What's in v3
- **Top-down view: the phone is held flat above your hand.** You look straight down at the floor. Your own fist is at the bottom of the screen, fingers pointing up, with the forearm coming in from the bottom edge, as if the screen were a window right above your hand. Swipe UP, tap, or flick the phone forward to throw: the yo-yo drops straight down away from you, shrinking as it falls and staying near your hand, with the string seen almost end-on. Pull DOWN, tap, or pull the phone back to bind. In your palm the yo-yo lies face-up; once thrown it turns edge-on (see below), with a capsule-shaped shadow on the floor below. The floor is the background, seen from above: wooden stage planks, a neon grid, or mown grass, each with a landing circle under your hand.
- **Hand that turns with the yo-yo.** The hand is always seen from above, the same way as the yo-yo. Holding it, your palm is up with the yo-yo resting face-up in it. On the throw your hand turns over (spring-animated) to show the back of the fist: knuckles in a row, fingers curled under, thumb tucked at the side, and the string dropping from under your fingers. It turns back palm-up as the yo-yo comes home to catch it. The view drifts with your hand so it stays near the centre.
- **Branding (v8).** The menu logo is an inline SVG wordmark: a chunky rounded gold "Y" with an ink outline, bevel lip and gloss band, and the "o" drawn as a glossy yo-yo with a gold rim, spinning swirl (paused for reduced motion), string and motion streaks. It takes the theme's accent colours (gold, cyan/magenta for Neon, peach/rose for Sunset). The app icons, favicon and social image use the same yo-yo emblem on a deep crimson spotlight gradient. The manifest lists 180/192/512 (also maskable)/1024 icons, and `og:image` / `twitter:image` point to `assets/og.png`.
- **Edge-on yo-yo (v8).** Out of the hand (throw, sleeper, swings, return) the yo-yo is seen from above the way a real one hangs under your hand: both halves facing down to the sides, each a domed, rounded rim with curvature shading in that design's colours, and a dark gap between them with the wound string and the string entering it. Spin shows as rim marks travelling over the rim, turning into streaks and a shimmer when it's fast. The axle runs left-right for a straight drop and turns smoothly to stay perpendicular to the direction of travel in a Whip, Around the World and Walk the Dog (where it rolls edge-on along the floor). It morphs face-up → edge-on on the throw and back on the catch, matching the hand turning over. Each design keeps its own edge look: tyre tread, brass step line, Flower petals, Diamond facets with sparkles, wood grain, pearl sheen, Night's neon rim. The menu hero shows the face plus a small edge-on inset. `__yo.yoView` exposes the morph (e) and axle angle (phi).
- **Info bubbles instead of on-screen instructions (v8).** The always-on hint line is gone. A small themed (i) at the end of the trick chips opens a bubble with what to do right now (the text follows the game state while open). Tap any trick chip for how to do that trick. Tap the X, outside, or press Esc to close; taps on the bubble never throw, and the tap that closes it doesn't throw either. First run only: the (i) pulses and one short tip shows itself before your first throw, then dismisses (saved as `seenInfo`). Score and trick popups stay as feedback.
- **Carnival mode** (on by default; switch it with the Free play / Carnival pill above PLAY, or in Settings). Carnival plates lie on the floor below you. Move your hand to aim, then drop a full sleeper, swing low or Walk the Dog across them to smash them, with shards, a burst, sound and bonus points. Small plates score more, gold plates are a rare bonus, some plates slide, and quick chains multiply up to ×4. `__yo.carn` exposes the plates and the chain.
- **Dynamic camera.** On a throw the lens zooms down after the yo-yo so it stays about the same size on screen. The floor grows as you close in, and your fingertips slide to the bottom edge. Walk the Dog zooms out to show your hand and the yo-yo rolling on the floor together, holds for a beat, then zooms back in and follows the yo-yo up to your hand. Swings get a moderate framing. All the moves are damped springs, the HUD never moves, and reduced motion tones it right down. `__yo.cam` exposes the camera mode and zoom.
- **Themes** (Settings > Theme): Gold Arena (default: bleachers with crowd lights, lighting truss, sweeping spotlights, perspective stage floor, vignette), Neon Night (synthwave sun, skyline, scrolling grid) and Sunset Park (sky, sun, hills, trees, grass). Each one restyles the HUD frames, buttons and accent colour too.
- **Game HUD.** Lilita One display font, a level badge with an XP bar, a coin counter, a combo chip that catches fire at ×2 and above, punch-zoom and screen shake on big tricks, and flame particles from combo ×3.
- **8 distinct yo-yos**, drawn procedurally in both the picker (3/4 view) and in play (face-on): Pocket, Maple, Brass, Hound, Orbit and Night, plus two SPECIAL ones. Whatever you pick is what you play with.
  - **Flower:** petal caps, pastel shell, petal trail. Unlock it by landing Rock the Baby 3 times.
  - **Diamond:** faceted crystal, glints, sparkle trail. Code only.
- **Redeem code** field in Settings and on the locked Diamond card. Input is normalised (all whitespace removed, upper-cased), hashed with SHA-256 via `crypto.subtle`, and compared to `1b53348b10abe5c76d83800e6779462bfa4ef26f5dc467e4972ae405dfc9b23a`. Only the hash is in this build. A match unlocks Diamond permanently (localStorage `yo-app-v3` -> `codes.diamond`) with a celebration; a miss shakes the field and shows an error. Resetting progress keeps redeemed codes.

## Debug / test hooks
- `window.__yo.state()` returns phase, model, theme, level/xp/coins, unlocked list, etc.
- `__yo.manual(true)` plus `__yo.advance(frames)` step the game frame by frame (used to record anim/throw-and-return.mp4).
- `__yo.drive(x, y)` and `__yo.release()` move the hand.
- `__yo.previewModel(id)` swaps the in-play art for this session only (nothing is unlocked or saved), `__yo.carnival(on)` toggles plates, `__yo.yoScreen()` gives the yo-yo's screen position (used for the edge-on lineup crops).
- `__yo.lineup()` renders every model's art into a PNG data URL. It is a preview only and unlocks nothing.
